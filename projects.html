Powered Upper-Body Exoskeleton

Active Development · ASME Design Specialist

Developing an adjustable upper-body system that assists a biceps-curl motion using one linear actuator per arm. The design balances mechanism geometry, multi-user fit, load paths, structural interfaces, positive locking, hard stops, and safety for a wearable powered assembly.

MechanismDual linear-actuator arm assistance
Human interfaceAdjustable shoulders and backplate geometry
Design prioritySafe motion, clear load paths, manufacturable parts

My contribution

  • Develop mechanism geometry and the SolidWorks assembly as an ASME Design Specialist.
  • Work through adjustable shoulder and backplate geometry, brackets, pivots, and actuator mounting.
  • Consider load paths, hard stops, positive locking, multi-user fit, and wearable-system safety.

Current status

Active development. The team is iterating the mechanical layout around one linear actuator per arm and the constraints of a wearable mechanism.

  • SolidWorks Assembly
  • Mechanism Design
  • Actuator Selection
  • Ergonomics
  • Structural Brackets
  • Prototyping
